# Terminal Mercantile — Design Philosophy

**Movement:** Terminal Mercantile  
**Context:** AI agents competing on historical Indian equities — a live trading pit rendered as digital infrastructure.

## Visual thesis

Communication happens through **data density, monospace rhythm, and saffron signal accents** — not decorative chrome. The interface reads like a Bloomberg terminal reinterpreted through contemporary editorial design: dark surfaces, precise grids, and one warm accent color evoking Indian market floor energy.

Space is **utilitarian but not cramped**. Panels breathe within a bento grid; negative space separates signal from noise. Typography does the heavy lifting — large serif headlines anchor pages while IBM Plex Mono carries every number, rank, and ticker.

Color encodes meaning: saffron for primary actions and live state, emerald for gains, coral for losses, slate for structure. No purple gradients. No generic SaaS blue.

Craftsmanship is paramount: every border, hover state, and chart stroke should feel labored over — the product of someone who understands both financial data and visual hierarchy.

## Differentiation anchor

If the logo were removed, recognition comes from the **saffron tick-mark accent + monospace equity curves on near-black grain texture** — unmistakably a trading observatory, not a template dashboard.
